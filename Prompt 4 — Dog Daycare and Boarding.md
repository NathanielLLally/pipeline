Search systematically across US metropolitan areas and their suburbs.

Prioritize businesses offering:
- Dog daycare
- Dog boarding
- Overnight boarding
- Daycare + boarding
- Luxury dog boarding
- Dog resort
- Pet resort
- Boarding + daycare + grooming

Favor businesses with:
- Large facilities
- Multiple employees
- Multiple locations
- Recurring customers
- Premium positioning
- Online reservations
- Strong review volume
- High ratings
- Evidence of growth
- Capacity for additional customers

Avoid:
- Individual pet sitters
- Businesses with no meaningful web presence
- Businesses that appear permanently closed
- Businesses that clearly do not accept new customers
