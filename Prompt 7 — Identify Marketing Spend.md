Using the existing qualified prospect database, identify businesses that appear to be actively investing in customer acquisition.

For each high-priority business, look for observable signals such as:
- Google Ads
- Meta/Facebook advertising
- Promotional landing pages
- Dedicated lead-generation pages
- "Book a consultation" funnels
- Online booking
- Promotional offers
- Tracking/marketing technology
- Multiple location landing pages
- SEO-focused pages

Do not claim that a business is running paid advertising unless there is reliable evidence.

Store marketing signals separately:

marketing_active
google_ads_signal
meta_ads_signal
lead_form_present
booking_present
marketing_evidence
marketing_score

Prioritize businesses where there is evidence that customer acquisition is already important to the business.

Update existing records rather than creating duplicates.
