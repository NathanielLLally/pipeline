Begin prospect discovery using the highest-priority ICP: premium dog training businesses.

Focus exclusively on:
- Dog trainers
- Dog training companies
- Private dog trainers
- In-home dog trainers
- Board-and-train businesses
- Dog behavior specialists
- Aggression/reactivity specialists
- Puppy training businesses

Start with major US metropolitan markets.

For each metro:
1. Search the core city.
2. Search major surrounding suburbs.
3. Search affluent nearby communities.
4. Use multiple relevant Google Maps search terms.
5. Store every unique business in the managed database.
6. Deduplicate against existing records.
7. Record the exact query and geographic target that produced each business.

Prioritize businesses that appear established and commercially viable.

Do NOT expand into grooming, boarding, daycare, walking, or generic pet services yet.

Your goal for this phase is to build a strong seed dataset of premium dog-training prospects rather than maximum geographic coverage.
