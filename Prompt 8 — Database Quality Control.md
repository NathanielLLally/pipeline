Perform a quality-control pass over the managed prospect database.

Identify and resolve:

- Duplicate businesses
- Duplicate domains
- Duplicate phone numbers
- Closed businesses
- Permanently moved businesses
- Businesses outside the target geography
- Businesses outside the target ICP
- Missing categories
- Incorrect categories
- Incomplete addresses
- Invalid websites
- Obvious spam/listing artifacts

Use Google Maps and the business website to validate questionable records.

Never delete a potentially useful prospect solely because one data field is missing.

Instead, distinguish:

VALID
NEEDS_ENRICHMENT
LOW_PRIORITY
REJECTED
CLOSED

Preserve the original discovery source and search history for every record.

Do not erase useful historical information during deduplication.
